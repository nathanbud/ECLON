# Year-end close

Keep this note current when the feature changes.

**How the old system closes a year** (read from 2023 to 2025 in the legacy data): a computer-generated **Closing Entry**, legacy General Ledger type 99 (`transsum` module 1, code 99), dated 31 December but made weeks later (2025's on 11 February 2026), explanation `Closing Entries (Computer Generated) for Date Ending: 12/31/2025`, `TR_DOCNO` the control number in 8 digits with no star, `BATCH_NO` = `yyyyMMdd` of 31 December.
For each department it debits every income account (4xxxx) and credits every expense account (5xxxx) to bring them to zero for the year, and puts the department's difference on **30198 Undivided net surplus** (`transdt` adjustment flag 1, update tag 1; encoded and posted by one person, approved by another).
It also writes zero lines for every other account (they change nothing) and, in `gltrans` only, one-sided type 99 rows on **30199 Net surplus for the period** (`branches.ProfitLossAcct_Code`) that cancel the year's daily type 98 "Profit Loss Summ" rows on that account.
Years before 2023 have no closing entry. The old database keeps no closing date; a year counts as closed once it has a closing entry (`ClosedBooks`).
There is no month-end closing in the old data.

**In this application** (`ph.coop.core.accounting.close`, `/api/year-end-close`, accountants and administrators, Accounting > Year-end close, `YearEndCloseTab.tsx`; PostgreSQL `V023` applied, MySQL `V060` written, not applied):
- `GET /preview?branchCode=&year=` works out the closing from `gltrans` (accounts by number 4xxxx and 5xxxx, as the old system; three loan accounts carry an income or expense type in the chart by mistake and are not closed; types 98 and 99 left out) and lists what blocks it (`YearEndRules.problems`): the year has not ended (by the application clock), it is already closed, a later year is closed, an earlier year is still open (years close in order), a journal dated in the year is not posted, the surplus account (`coop.year-end.surplus-account`, default 30198) is missing or switched off, or there is nothing to close.
- `POST` prepares it: a row in `ecis_year_end_close` (PREPARED) with the surplus, the line count and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the lines; nothing is written to the ledger. At most one closing that is not cancelled per branch and year. Audited `yearend.prepare`.
- `POST /{id}/post` by a different person (both must be enabled users of the old system) checks again, refuses if the fingerprint no longer matches (something was booked into the year after it was prepared), then writes the Closing Entry as above (one header; zero lines left out) plus the one-sided 30199 rows that cancel exactly the year's type 98 rows per department, marks the year's OPEN and SOFT_CLOSED accounting periods CLOSED (each audited), and, when the year's balances were already built (`ecis_gl_year_balance`), adds the closing to them. Audited `yearend.post`.
- `POST /{id}/cancel` with a reason, only while PREPARED. Audited `yearend.cancel`.
- A closed year is never reopened. Journals (`JournalController.periodFor`) and memos (`MemoController.requireOpenPeriod`) refuse a date in a closed year.

**Closed periods refuse every posting** (`PostingPeriods.requireOpen(jdbc, branch, date)`, static like `AuditLog.write`): a date in a closed year, or in an accounting period that is SOFT_CLOSED, CLOSED or LOCKED, is refused with 409 and a message naming the period. A day no period covers is allowed (periods need not cover every day; journals and memos are stricter). It is called just before the header is written in `MemberLedgerPoster` (savings, share capital, loan payments and releases, time deposit placement, voucher pay-outs), `BankCashController`, `ChequeReturnController`, `PenaltyWaiverController`, `ShareWithdrawalController`, `TimeDepositClosureController`, `HandoverController`, `LoanApplicationWorkflowController`, and at the start of the back-dated runs: savings interest (quarter end), time deposit accruals (period end) and dividends (cut-off date). The old permission `access_trans.CAN_BACKDATE_CLOSEDBOOKS` is not honoured: nobody posts into a closed period. Tested in `SavingsLedgerPostingTest`.

**Reports changed with it**: the Statement of Operations leaves out types 98 and 99, so a year that was closed shows its real income and expenses (2025 used to show a net of -3,030; it now shows the surplus of 237,927,903.80). The Statement of Financial Condition and the trial balance leave out type 98 and the type 99 rows on the net surplus for the period account (the trial balance for 2025 was 237.9 million out; it now balances).

**Not built**: month-end closing and a closing checklist (drawers counted, vouchers and cheques settled, accruals run, bank reconciled); sharing out the surplus to the statutory funds (30301 to 30304), interest on share capital and patronage refunds (done with journals and the Dividends screen).

Tested by `YearEndRulesTest` and `YearEndCloseTest` (closes the real 2026 as if on 20 January 2027 and rolls back; takes about 20 seconds).
