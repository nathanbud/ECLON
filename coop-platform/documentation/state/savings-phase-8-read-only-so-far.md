# Savings (phase 8, read-only so far)

Moved from CLAUDE.md unchanged (one sentence per line). Keep this note current when the feature changes.

**Savings (phase 8, read-only so far)**: legacy savings is subsidiary ledger class 22: accounts in `sd` (types: SD - Regular, Associate, Kiddies, Kaya; rate in `SDINT_RATE`), holds in `hold` (reasons in `hold_code`: collateral, earmarked, lost passbook, garnished, maintaining balance, TD certificate), movements in `sldtl` event 11 (credits negative, balance = -SUM(AMT)).
Typical transaction types on a savings account: Cash Receipt (10), Deposit Withdrawal (23), Interest (Savings) (53), Dividends (54), journals and Remittance Journals. `GET /api/members/{b}/{c}/savings` and `.../savings/{type}/{ref}` (history with running balance and holds; available = balance minus all holds, never below zero); Savings tab on the client profile. `SDSTATUS` 15 Active, 16 Dormant, 17 Closed are inferred from the data (no lookup table).
The GL account comes from `glcontrol` (class 22, event 11, number = status): 2010101 for types 1 to 3, 2010106 for Kaya; accrued interest uses event 75 and 2011603. **Time deposits (`td`, class 24) are read only so far** (`TimeDepositController`, Time Deposits tab on the member profile, `documentation/time-deposit-map.md`): a certificate's money is ledger event 11 and year-end accrued interest event 71, and its master row is never closed when it is paid out, so status comes from the ledger; interest at maturity is shown as an estimate (rate x term days / 360; 72% of real rollovers credit that less a flat 10.00). **Placing a certificate is built** (`TimeDepositPlacementController`, a Cash Receipt with the certificate credited to its type account, a 25.00 fee to 442202 and new `slmastersum` and `td` rows; the cashier types the rate and a supervisor allows one outside what was used for the term, kind `TD_RATE`; needs `V027_time_deposit_placement.sql`, INSERT on `td`, and `V028_td_trigger_definer.sql`, which re-creates the old BEFORE INSERT trigger `update_td_certno` (it sets `TDCERT_NUM` = `TDREF_NO`) with a definer that exists, because its original definer `root@%` is not on this server; both applied by an administrator). **Payout and renewal of a matured Short Term certificate are built and always by hand** (`TimeDepositClosureController` encodes the old back-office Cash Voucher: certificate, accrued interest (event 71) and the rest of the term interest to 50105 debited, vouchers payable or a new certificate credited, with a 10.00 renewal fee to 442202; approved and posted on the Disburse tab by two other people; `TimeDepositClosureTest`). **Nobody and nothing rolls over by itself**: the Front Office **TD Maturities** tab (`TimeDepositMaturityController`, badge on the tab and on the Front Office tile) lists matured and soon-maturing certificates with Pay out and Renew buttons. **The interest accrual run is built** (`TimeDepositAccrualController`, module 8 type 52: Lock In every month end, Short Term every December 31, event 71 credited per certificate and 50105 debited per department; preview, encode, approve, post by three people, started by hand on the Front Office TD Accruals tab; `documentation/time-deposit-accrual-map.md`, `TimeDepositAccrualPostingTest`, `TimeDepositAccrualRealDataTest`). **Lock In payout and renewal at maturity are built** through the same controller (interest = balance x rate% for each unpaid year, the clerk picks the years; the monthly accrual is taken back and the difference goes to 50105, a credit when over-accrued; no renewal fee; a Lock In may be renewed into Short Term or Lock In; mapped in the Lock In section of `documentation/time-deposit-map.md`). **Pre-termination is built** (a running certificate, button Pre-terminate on the member's Time Deposits tab: the whole certificate debited and optionally part re-placed, a 100.00 charge for Short Term or 1%/5% of the principal for Lock In to 442201, Short Term interest at 1% for the days held, Lock In interest none unless typed, the accrual taken back; mapped in `documentation/time-deposit-map.md`, tested in `TimeDepositClosureTest`). **Lock In interest withdrawn on an anniversary is built** (button Withdraw interest on a running Lock In: a voucher that debits the accrual and 50105 and credits vouchers payable, the principal untouched, limited to a year of interest per anniversary passed less what was withdrawn).
Not built: partial withdrawals of principal that keep a certificate running. **Savings deposits and withdrawals are posted** (`POST /api/front-office/savings/deposits`, `/withdrawals/check`, `/withdrawals`; Deposit and Withdraw buttons in the Front Office **Member Accounts** tab (`SavingsTellerPanel`, default export `MemberAccountsPanel`)), written by `MemberLedgerPoster` exactly like the real Cash Receipt (module 3 type 10) and Deposit Withdrawal (module 2 type 23) mapped in `documentation/savings-posting-map.md`; share payments use the same writer.
A withdrawal is refused over the balance or the available balance (after holds) and on dormant or closed accounts.
A withdrawal that leaves the balance under the account minimum (`SDMIN_BAL`), or is over the cashier's `AccessDisbursementLimit`/`AccessMaxWithdrawal`, needs a supervisor approval: the cashier asks through `ecis_fo_override` (kinds `SAV_MIN_BALANCE`, `SAV_DISB_LIMIT`, text from `/withdrawals/check`), a different supervisor approves, and posting spends that approval once (`used_ref`, V012); each use is audited.
The old system logged these in `actvtlog`; the app has no write access there, so the audit log is used instead. **Interest on deposits is mapped and the calculation reproduced, not posted** (`SavingsInterest`, `documentation/savings-interest-map.md`): quarterly A.D.B. (end-of-day balances summed over the quarter, interest = sum x type rate / 36,500, half up, nothing under a 500 A.D.B.), the rate is the loan type's `sltype` rate and not the account's; `SavingsInterestRealDataTest` matches 359 of 360 posted amounts exactly. **The quarterly interest run is built** (`SavingsInterestController`, `SavingsInterestRun`: preview, encode vouchers per type tag 2, approve by a second person, post by a third, refused if the quarter already has type 53 vouchers; accounts with a zero ending balance are skipped, accounts with 100,000 or more interest are held back; `SavingsInterestRunTest` passes, post step included (rerun 2026-10-21)). **The annual dividend and patronage refund run is built** (`DividendRun`, `DividendController`, Front Office Dividends tab, module 6 type 54: 8% of the average of the 12 month-end share capital balances of the year before the cut-off plus 8.23% of the loan interest, service fees and event 24 charges paid that year, fitted to the cent for 99.9% of the real 2026 members; the rates and the mutual benefit and hospitalization amounts are typed by the accountant because the old rules for the insurance amounts are not known; the rest goes to savings or share capital; preview, encode, approve by a second person, post by a third, once a year; `documentation/dividend-map.md`, `DividendRunRulesTest`, `DividendRunRealDataTest`, `DividendRunTest`).
Not built: cheque deposits, holds, reactivating dormant accounts (dormant accounts are refused until it is known how the old system treats them), time deposits.
