# Loan interest and penalty worksheet, and how documents are made

**Documents are made on the server from one computed result, in two ways:** fixed-layout PDFs with **JasperReports 7.0.8** (template `backend/coop-core/src/main/resources/reports/loan-worksheet.jrxml`, the Jasper 7 format, editable in Jaspersoft Studio; fonts from `jasperreports-fonts`, DejaVu Sans embedded) and Excel files with **Apache POI 5.5.1** (real numbers and dates in separate columns). Jasper compiles and fills on JDK 25 (checked). Use this pair for new documents (statements, receipts, financial statements): compute once in plain code, test the numbers, then feed both writers.

## The worksheet (`/api/members/{b}/{c}/loans/{type}/{ref}/worksheet/pdf|xlsx?asOf=yyyy-MM-dd`)
- `LoanWorksheet` (pure calculation), `LoanWorksheetService` (reads `loan`, `sldtl` events 11/23/41, `amortsched`, `branches`, `client`), `LoanWorksheetDocuments` (PDF and Excel), `LoanWorksheetController` (downloads; each is audited as `loan.worksheet.pdf|xlsx` and logged in `ecis_data_export`). Screen: **Worksheet as of** date, PDF and Excel buttons at the foot of the Loans Profile dialog.
- Rules, fitted to the old system's printed worksheet (`pics/intwork.pdf`, loan 1221-00003212 as of 2026-04-24) and matching it to the centavo, every row and total: interest accrues on the principal balance before the row for the calendar days since the previous accrual row, x rate / 365, rounded half up. A row accrues when it is a principal payment, a scheduled due date or the cutoff; an interest-only payment (event 23 alone) is credited without a new accrual, so an advance shows as a negative balance in parentheses. Should-be balance = `amortsched.PRINBAL` on the latest due date up to the row. Delayed principal = actual balance above it; below it is "Over/Advance principal". Status is ADVANCE PAYMENT when paid ahead and nothing is late.
- **Penalty is NOT confirmed.** The sample loan was never late, so all its penalty figures are zero. The penalty columns use the delayed principal of the previous row x the penalty rate x days / 365, with no grace days taken off. The old system's real penalty rule is only partly known (`loan-posting-map.md`, "Penalty fit"); do not rely on the penalty columns until a worksheet of a late loan has been compared.
- The cooperative's full name is not stored in the database; it is read from `coopsystem_report_header.coop_name`, then the branding name (Settings > System > Sign-in screen), then `coop.name`, then the branch acronym. **Set it in the sign-in screen settings.**
- Tests: `LoanWorksheetTest` (the printed figures, a late loan), `LoanWorksheetDocumentsTest` (PDF and Excel), `LoanWorksheetRealDataTest` (tag `realdata`: the real loan from the database, downloads, audit and export-log rows, refused dates).
