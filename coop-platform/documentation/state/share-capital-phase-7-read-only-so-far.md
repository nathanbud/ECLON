# Share capital (phase 7, read-only so far)

Moved from CLAUDE.md unchanged (one sentence per line). Keep this note current when the feature changes.

**Share capital (phase 7, read-only so far)**: legacy share capital is subsidiary ledger class 31: accounts in `sc` (type names in `sltype`: Paid up Share Cap, Dep for Cap Subs, preferred types), movements in `sldtl` (credits negative, no stored balance; balance = -SUM(AMT) per type and reference number), event 11. `GET /api/members/{b}/{c}/shares` and `.../shares/{type}/{ref}` (ledger history with running balance); the client profile has a Share Capital tab. `sc.SCSTATUS` 15 and 17 are shown as Active and Closed, inferred from the data (no legacy lookup table). **Share payments are posted** (`POST /api/front-office/share-payments`, the Post payment button on the Share Capital tab): a Cash Receipt (module 3, type 10) written exactly like the real one mapped in `documentation/share-payment-posting-map.md` (transsum, 2 transdt, 1 sldtl, 2 gltrans; never slsum, sc or denom*), dated today, cash into the poster's own drawer.
Needs V011 (INSERT on `sldtl`), a drawer, a department and `access_trans` CAN_NEW for type 10. Refused for non-active accounts, balance over `SCMAX_BAL` (the old override flow is not wired in), and a repeat for the same client within 60 seconds unless confirmed.
Receipt number: typed (up to 8 letters/digits) or `*` plus 7 digits (the real source of the old pre-printed numbers is still unknown). **Share withdrawals are encoded** (`POST /api/front-office/share-withdrawals`, Withdraw button on the Share Capital tab, `ShareWithdrawalController`): a back-office Cash Voucher (module 2, type 21, tag 2) debiting the share capital and crediting the member's vouchers-payable account, mapped in `documentation/share-withdrawal-posting-map.md`; approved and posted on Disburse by `LoanReleaseController` (three different people), tested by `ShareWithdrawalPostingTest`. **Paying the cash out of vouchers payable is built** (`VoucherPayoutController`, Cash Voucher payment module 2 type 24, the whole balance from the cashier's own drawer, posted at once; see the Cash and teller entry).
Not built: cheque payments, subscriptions, certificates.
New control numbers use `Db.nextControlNumber` (`ORDER BY ... LIMIT 1 FOR UPDATE`, PostgreSQL-safe), not `MAX() ... FOR UPDATE`.
