# Loans (phase 10, read-only so far)

Moved from CLAUDE.md unchanged (one sentence per line). Keep this note current when the feature changes.

**Loans (phase 10, read-only so far)**: a loan is a row of `loan` (43k rows; type names in `sltype` class 12, purpose in `loanpurpose`, collateral in `collateraltype`, term unit in `term`, payment mode in `paymentmode`, amortization in `amortype`); its ledger is class 12 of `sldtl` where **event 11 is principal (release = debit, payments = credits, so outstanding = the plain sum)**, 23 interest paid, 31 service fee (deducted at release), and others for accrued interest and penalties; the repayment schedule is `amortsched` (580k rows: `DUEDATE`, `PRINDUE`, `INTDUE`, `PRINBAL` = balance after); co-makers are in `comaker`.
Applications are `loanapp` (32k, statuses New, Verified, Approved, Disapproved, Cancelled, Deferred, RELEASED in `loanappstatus`). `GET /api/members/{b}/{c}/loans` (`?asOf=`, `?includePaid=`) and `.../loans/{type}/{ref}`; Loans tab on the client profile.
Arrears are principal only: principal due by the date minus principal paid (released minus outstanding), and days late of the oldest unpaid instalment. **`LoanSTATUS` is 11 current, 12 past due, 13 in litigation, 14 restructured** (the number `glcontrol` keys the loan's GL account by).
The old data stops on 2026-04-24, so tests measure arrears from that date.
Not built: applications and approval, release, payments with interest/fee/penalty rules, restructuring, collection, provisioning.
The release and the payment are mapped in `documentation/loan-posting-map.md`: a release is a back-office Cash Voucher (type 21; one debit for the loan, credits for the service fee, deductions and the net proceeds into a member vouchers-payable account); a payment is a Cash Receipt with interest-first split, interest = outstanding x rate x days since last payment / 365 (verified on two loans; 2024 payments used 360). **Loan cash payments are posted** (`POST /api/front-office/loan-payments` and `/check`; Pay button on the Loans tab) through `MemberLedgerPoster.postVoucher` (cash line plus a principal line, event 11 with the loan status as `DTCTS_NUM`, and an interest line, event 23, number 99; ledger rows get the next sequence over all events; the first use of an event on an account adds its `slsum` row, which needs V014).
Interest is `LoanRules.interest` (365-day year, plain rounding: exact on 14 of 18 real payments, one cent off on the rest). **Penalties:** a loan late beyond its grace period carries a penalty, taken first (then interest, then principal) and posted as an event 41 line, number 99 (`LoanPaymentController`, `LoanRules.penalty`).
The program only **suggests** the amount (penalty mode 4: unpaid scheduled principal plus interest of each due instalment x rate x days since its due date / 365; exact in 72% of simple cases and about 6% overall, see `documentation/loan-posting-map.md`); the cashier may type another figure, and any figure different from the suggestion (every figure when the loan's penalty mode is not 4) needs a supervisor's approval through `ecis_fo_override` (kind `LOAN_PENALTY`, spent once on posting, audit action `loan.payment.override.used`).
Tested in `LoanPaymentPostingTest` and `LoanRulesTest`.
Refused: loans in litigation, amounts over the payoff plus penalty, a penalty larger than the payment, closed clients. `Clock` is a bean so tests can fix the date (old data stops on 2026-04-24). **Penalty waivers:** a Penalties waived report (payments charged below the suggestion, from the audit log) and the accountant's waiver journal (debit a `41499xx` expense account, credit the loan as event 42, a second accountant or administrator approves) are on Accounting > Penalty waivers (`PenaltyWaiverController`, `PenaltyWaiverTest`); the journal is a memo and does not change what the member owes.
Not built: the exact penalty formula (partial payments, penalty modes 1, 5 and 6), penalty waivers as a reduction of the amount owed (the old ledger has none; see below), payments by cheque, journal or remittance. **Release, pure parts only so far:** `LoanSchedule` (monthly, quarterly, lump-sum diminishing-balance schedules; matches 599 of 600 real ones, checked by `LoanScheduleRealDataTest`; **annuity** (`amortype` 3, level payment = P r / (1 - (1+r)^-n) with r = rate x 30 x months per payment / 36500, interest on the balance rounded half up, last row clears the balance; loan types with `ND_FLAG` 1 charge 30 days a month, `ND_FLAG` 2 counts real days; monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and lump sum; matches about 98% of the 2,240 real annuity schedules from 2022 on, the rest were changed after they were made; the Salary Loan is the main user) so the application screen now accepts diminishing-balance and annuity types, and the Loan Profile checks both; straight line and the AnnuityRate kinds are not mapped and `ReleasePlan` (voucher lines with clerk-typed deductions, balance check); the release itself is now **approving and posting the voucher the old system already encoded** (`LoanReleaseController`, `/api/front-office/loan-releases`: pending list, approve, post; encoder, approver and poster must differ; needs type 21 CAN_NEW; `V015_loan_release_post.sql` = column UPDATE on `transdt` DTAPPRVBY/DTPOSTBY/DTUPD_TAG, applied to `iaccsbsucmpc01`; `LoanReleasePostingTest` passes and rolls back).
The ledger date of a posted release (today) is assumed, not verified. **Loan application (status New) is created** by `LoanApplicationController` (`/api/members/{b}/{c}/loan-applications`, `GET /template`, `POST`; New application dropdown on the Loans tab, `LoanApplicationDialog`): inserts the loan account row in `slmastersum` (a foreign key needs it first), `loanapp`, `loan`, `amortsched`, `loandeduc`, `comaker` and an audit row in one transaction; needs `AccessCanAddLoanApplication`, the member's vouchers-payable account, deductions below the amount; `V016_loan_application.sql` (INSERT grants, applied by an admin by hand) and `LoanApplicationCreateTest` (rolls back, 4 pass). **Verify and approve (V017 applied, `LoanWorkflowTest` 5 pass):** `LoanApplicationWorkflowController` (`/api/loan-applications`: pending, detail, verify, approve; panel `LoanApplicationsPanel` on Front Office > Disburse) needs `AccessCanVerify/ApproveLoanApplication`; approving creates the type 21 release voucher (tag 2, encoded by the application's encoder, number in `loanapp.TR_CTLNO`, GL accounts from `glcontrol`) which `LoanReleaseController` then approves and posts.
Segregation: verifier and approver differ from the encoder, the approver from the verifier (read from the audit log), and the application's approver cannot approve its voucher. **Single-person exemption:** a system administrator can let one named person (never themselves) do every step alone, with a reason, audited (`SingleHandGrants`, `/api/admin/single-hand-loans`, table `ecis_single_hand`); Users screen has a "Loans alone" switch with a reason dialog. `V017_loan_workflow.sql` (table plus column UPDATE on `loanapp`) was applied by an administrator by hand; `LoanWorkflowTest` skips if MySQL is back in `read_only` (`SET GLOBAL read_only=OFF` as root). **Cancel** (`POST /api/loan-applications/{id}/cancel`, button in the panel): status 5 with the date and person in `APPRV_DATE`/`APPRVBY` like the old system, reason in the audit log, refused once the voucher is posted; a cancelled application's unposted voucher can no longer be approved or posted. **Disapprove (4) is built** (`POST /api/loan-applications/{id}/disapprove`, button in the panel): from New or Verified, by someone with the approve right who did not encode it, with date and person in `APPRV_DATE`/`APPRVBY` like the two real ones (2023, 2024) and the reason in the audit log.
Defer (6) is not built: no real example exists.
An application now **opens the member's vouchers-payable account (29/1: `slmastersum` + `osl`, status 15, amount 0.00) when they have none**, as the old system does (needs `V018_open_vouchers_payable.sql`, INSERT on `osl`, applied by an admin; until then such applications fail); share capital and mutual assistance accounts are opened with the member's account in the old system, so an application with a deduction into a missing one is refused. **Pay-off, renewal and restructuring:** an application may pay off the member's earlier loans (principal, interest, typed penalty; `loandeduc` behavior 4); approval turns them into credit lines on the old loan (`DTCTS_NUM` = its status, GL from `glcontrol`) and posting clears it (`LoanPayoffTest`); classification New / Renewal / Restructured is chosen, and Restructured needs a pay-off and records `LoanRESTRUCT`/`LoanXSLT_CODE`/`LoanXREF_NO`.
Not built: opening those accounts, loans with unearned interest, penalty calculation. **Loan Profile (read only, stage 1 of the application screen):** `GET /api/members/{b}/{c}/loans/{type}/{ref}/profile` (`LoanProfileController`) and the Profile button on the Loans tab (`LoanProfileDialog`) show the old Loans Profile layout (header, Setup, Due Dates, Co-Makers, Collateral) and check the stored schedule against `LoanSchedule`; joint type 3 = NONE and diminish 1 = E-DueDate are inferred (no lookup table); pledge profile names are not looked up yet.
Loan release screen: Front Office > Disburse tab (`LoanReleasePanel`). **Co-maker amounts and holds:** each co-maker may agree an amount held on their savings or share capital (`ecis_loan_comaker`, `V019_comaker_holds.sql`, applied by an administrator; INSERT on `hold`); the `hold` row is written when the release is posted (`CoMakerHolds`, tested in `LoanWorkflowTest`); `comaker.ClientIDCoMaker` is the borrower and `CoMakerClientID` the co-maker (see `documentation/loan-posting-map.md`).
Releasing holds is not built. **Loan setup screen** (administrators only, `LoanSetupController`, `/api/admin/loan-setup`, tile Loan Setup, `LoanSetupPage`): create, edit and disable loan types (`sltype` class 12; a new type is a copy of an existing one and also copies its `glcontrol` accounts and `loansldeduc` deductions), and add, rename and delete (when unused) loan purposes and collateral types; each change needs a reason and writes an audit row with the values before and after; loan types are never deleted; changes reach new applications only. `V020_loan_setup.sql` (INSERT/UPDATE grants, applied) and `LoanSetupTest` (8 pass, rolls back).
Not covered: terms, payment modes, amortization kinds, loan classes, and the deduction formulas. **Co-maker holds setup:** a fourth tab on that screen (`HoldSetupController`, `/api/admin/hold-setup`, table `ecis_hold_setting`, `V021_hold_setup.sql` applied) says which savings and share capital types can take a co-maker hold and which is suggested first (Paid up Share Cap to begin with); only allowed types are offered.
A member's share capital and savings tabs show a note when money is held because they are a co-maker (`GET /api/members/{b}/{c}/comaker-holds`, which also lists amounts agreed on applications not released yet). **Share capital withdrawals now refuse what is on hold** (the account loader had share capital holds hard-wired to 0; `HoldSetupTest`, `ShareWithdrawalPostingTest`).
Older open points: the `loan`/`amortsched`/`loanapp`/`slmastersum` (member vouchers-payable account, class 29) writes and their grants (a V015), who approves, and the share-capital deduction formulas (`loansldeduc` + VBScript in `codestorage` do not reproduce real amounts).
See `documentation/loan-posting-map.md`.
