# Membership lifecycle (full CRUD)

Moved from CLAUDE.md unchanged (one sentence per line). Keep this note current when the feature changes.

**Membership lifecycle (full CRUD)**: read and profile edit already existed; **register** and **close** are now maker-checker too (`MemberLifecycleController`, `/api/member-requests`, table `ecis_member_request`, `V037_member_lifecycle.sql` applied; INSERT on `client`, `sc`, `sd`).
A person with edit permission asks, a Membership Officer who is not them approves (reject needs a reason, the requester can withdraw).
Approving a registration inserts `client` (next number per branch, check digit `LifecycleRules.checkDigit`, fits all 100,614 members) and the accounts the old system opens (Regular: 22/1, 31/1, 29/5, 29/6; Associate: 22/2, 31/3); only those two member types. **There is no hard delete: closing sets status 3 with `ClosedDate`/`ClosedReason`/ClientType 6 or 7**, refused unless every ledger class is at zero (loans may remain only for Bad debts) and no loan application is in progress. **Reactivation** (`/reactivations`, kind REACTIVATE): dormant or closed members come back on the same record (never deceased; Bad debts/Offset closures also need an administrator); accounts still active are kept and missing ones opened new. **Returning members and share capital** is an administrator switch (`MembershipPolicyController`, `ecis_setting`, `V038_system_setting.sql` applied, Settings > Membership > Membership policy, off by default): when on, a reactivation cannot be approved until the active share account holds the type's minimum.
Screens: **Photo and fingerprints can be taken during registration** (`BiometricStaging`, table `ecis_member_request_item`, `V042_registration_photo_fingerprint.sql` applied: the photo and consent are held with the request, fingerprint templates encrypted and bound to the request; a finger already enrolled or held for another registration is refused at once via `POST /api/member-requests/biometric-check`, when the request is sent and again at approval; approval moves them to the member's `image`, consent and fingerprint records, rejecting or withdrawing wipes them; needs the edit-images permission; `RegistrationBiometricsTest`). **Register new member opens the member profile itself, blank and in edit mode** (`NewMemberScreen` in `ClientProfilePage`: the Info tabs plus Groups, same fields; `GET /api/member-requests/template` gives the empty tabs; Send for approval posts the draft as a REGISTER request), Close membership, Reactivate membership, Approvals > New members and closings.
Mapped in `documentation/membership-lifecycle-map.md`; tested by `LifecycleRulesTest` and `MemberLifecycleTest`.
