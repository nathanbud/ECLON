# Cash and teller (phase 9)

Moved from CLAUDE.md unchanged (one sentence per line). Keep this note current when the feature changes.

**Cash and teller (phase 9)**: **Vouchers payable are paid out in cash** (`VoucherPayoutController`, `/api/front-office/voucher-payouts`, panel Pay out vouchers payable on Front Office > Disburse): the old Cash Voucher payment (module 2, type 24) is a payable debit plus the cashier's cash credit, the whole balance, written by one cashier and posted at once; refused when the cashier's drawer holds less (the app's own rule) and a payment over the cashier's limit needs a supervisor (kind `VOUCHER_DISB_LIMIT`); mapped in `documentation/voucher-payout-map.md`, tested by `VoucherPayoutTest`; not built: the mixed multi-account type 24 vouchers. cash drawers, hand-offs (cash transfer), limits, blind end-of-day counts with variance, and supervisor overrides already exist in the front office window. **Only `transdt` lines tagged `DTUPD_TAG` 1 or 5 are in the general ledger** (their sum equals `gltrans` exactly for the account checked; tag NULL and 2 lines are not posted).
Banks: `bankcode` lists the bank accounts (`BCID`, name, account no., ledger account `BCCOAID`); the ledger side is class 11 type 2, events 61 (current) and 62 (savings), `DTCTS_NUM` = bank id; Bank Deposit and Bank Withdrawal are types 33 and 35 in module 5. The old system has no reconciliation data (`bankrecon_setup` empty, `DTISCLEARED` unused), so reconciliation is new: `ecis_bank_recon` and `ecis_bank_recon_item` (V013) hold a statement balance on a date plus the ledger entries still outstanding; book balance (from `gltrans`) minus outstanding must equal the statement balance, and a different person finalizes it (`/api/banks`, Accounting > Banks tab, ACCOUNTANT or ADMIN).
Outstanding entries carry over to the next statement; legacy rows are never changed. **Bank deposits and withdrawals of vault cash are posted** (`BankCashController`, `/api/front-office/bank-cash`, Bank deposit panel on the Handover tab, head cashier only; vault 10001 event 50 against the bank ledger account from `glcontrol` class 11 type 2 event 61/62 with number = bank id; count in `denomsum`; mapped in `documentation/bank-cash-posting-map.md`, tested by `BankCashPostingTest`). **Deposits of vault cheques are built** (`cheques: true` on `/bank-cash/deposits`, 100111 sub-ledger type 3 event 51, no count; panel on the Handover tab). **Bank charges and interest need no new feature**: the old system books them only inside the accountant's month-end Bank Reconciliation General Journal (bank vs 440501 interest income or 50317 charges), which the app's journals already cover (mapped in `bank-cash-posting-map.md`).
**Opening a drawer** at the start of the business day is built (see `business-day.md`). **Other receipts, mixed payments, Cash Vouchers and Check Vouchers** are posted from a front office draft (see `documentation/general-voucher-map.md`), so the mixed multi-account type 24 payments are no longer missing.
