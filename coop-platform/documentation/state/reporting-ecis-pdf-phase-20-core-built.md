# Reporting (ECIS.pdf phase 20, core built)

Moved from CLAUDE.md unchanged (one sentence per line). Keep this note current when the feature changes.

**Reporting (ECIS.pdf phase 20, core built)**: `ph.coop.core.report.Reports` framework (a report declares parameters and returns a typed table) with `ReportController` (`GET /api/reports`, `GET /api/reports/{id}`; role-filtered; every run audited as `report.run`) and the Reports tile (`ReportsPage`, print and CSV): membership (status, growth), loans (portfolio, status, PAR, delinquent, maturity, releases, payments, collection; `LoanReports` in the loan package reuses `LoanRules.arrears`; on 2026-04-24 it finds 10,512 loans, 2,699,050,350.25 outstanding, PAR over 30 days 31.47%), general ledger of one account, inventory (balance, movement, low stock, valuation, count variance) and POS (daily, by product, by store, returns, discounts); `ReportsTest`; see `documentation/reporting-design.md`.
Not built: cash flow, CIC reports, PDF/scheduled output. **The guide's body has 33 phases, not the 21 of its summary list** (16 Document Management, 17 Workflow Engine, 18 Audit and Compliance, 19 Data Privacy, 21 Management Dashboard, 22 Data Warehouse, 23-27 testing, 28 Data Migration, 29-33 production): see the reporting design note.

**Aging of receivables** (Reports > Receivables > Aging of receivables, menu entry `aging`, view `reports` tab `aging`; administrators and accountants): the old system's AGING OF RECEIVABLES FILTER (`pics/reports.png`) rebuilt. `ReceivablesAgingController` (`GET /api/reports/receivables-aging/options`, `POST /api/reports/receivables-aging`, audited as `report.run` with the choices) loads the receivable classes 12 (loans), 13 (accounts receivable) and 25 (investment deposit plan, empty in the old data) from `sldtl` (event 11 = principal, balance = plain sum; last transaction = the account's latest entry of any event), the account rows (`loan`, `ar`: release and maturity dates and the loan-side codes), `amortsched` for days late (`LoanRules.arrears`, as the loan reports) and the client for the client-side codes. `ReceivablesAging` (pure) ages each account by PAR (days the oldest unpaid instalment is late) or by maturity (days past the maturity date), puts it in one of seven buckets (current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 91-180, 181-365, over 365), counts its whole balance at risk from the PAR @ / maturity @ days, nests up to three groupings (SL class, SL type, aging bucket, SL status, client department, collector (the client's, else the one of the client's area in `geo`), geographical area, employer, loan purpose, loan class, borrower class, collateral type, pay mode, collection type, DOSRI tag, gender, client type, client account status, client) with a subtotal per group, sorts the accounts (name, number, balance, days aged, maturity, release) and can leave them out (Show totals only). Filters: cut-off date, released, maturity and last transaction ranges, SL classes and types, outstanding and days-aged ranges. The old screen's Firm, Major/Sub/Specific class, Assessment, Security type, Client rating and CI groupings are left out: their tables are empty or the field is free text. Accounts with no repayment schedule count as current under PAR (the note says how many). The screen (`reports/ReceivablesAging.tsx`, helpers `reports/aging.ts`) has tabs Basic, Groupings and Number filters, a summary (outstanding, past due, at risk, ratio), the spread as a coloured bar, a table whose groups fold (big grouped runs open folded at the innermost level; at most 1,500 lines drawn until Show all), CSV and print; the choices are remembered on the computer (not the cut-off date). A run takes about 10 seconds on the full data. The **Accrued Interest** tab (switches: Work out accrued interest, Include penalty (estimate), Only accounts that owe interest or penalty; request fields `accruedInterest`, `penalty`, `owingOnly`) adds per account and per group the interest due, interest paid ahead, penalty due and total owed (principal + interest + penalty), with tiles and columns, and the sorts Interest due and Total owed. `ReceivableAccrual` (pure) runs the confirmed worksheet rule (`LoanWorksheet.compute`: the yearly rate on the principal balance per calendar day over 365, less interest paid) on each account's rows of events 11, 22, 23, 41 and 42, read in one pass (`ORDER BY TR_DATE, SEQNO`), with the schedule balances (`amortsched.PRINBAL`) and the rates (`LoanINT_RATE`/`LoanPEN_RATE`, `ARINT_RATE`/`ARPEN_RATE`). Two additions found in the data, both beyond what the printed worksheet reads: about a third of outstanding loans (3,205 of 10,512 on 2026-04-24) had their interest taken at release as unearned interest (event 22 credit) and moved to income monthly (22 debit against 24 credit, not a payment), so 22 credits count as interest paid; and penalty waived (event 42 credits) settles penalty like event 41. The penalty rule is the worksheet's, not yet confirmed. On 2026-04-24 the whole book (about 10 s) shows 159.4 M interest due (102 M of it on loans over a year late; the median current loan owes 93.45), 4.7 M paid ahead, 157.2 M penalty. The interest follows the ledger's principal balance, so a loan whose principal was raised by a journal entry for a while (loan 5252-26-191: a 2,808,000 general journal on its release date) shows interest on it, as the old worksheet would. Loan 1221-00003212 matches the printed worksheet (interest due 6,809.59). Also tested by `ReceivableAccrualTest`. **Excel and PDF** (buttons Excel, PDF and CSV over the table; `POST /api/reports/receivables-aging/xlsx|pdf` with the same choices as the run, `ReceivablesAgingDocuments`, pure): the server keeps each person's last run for 10 minutes and reuses it when the choices are the same, so the file comes at once (other choices run again and are audited as `report.run`); each file is written to `ecis_data_export` and audited as `report.export`. The workbook (sheet Aging) has the cooperative and office on top, the header row frozen with a filter, numbers as numbers (amounts `#,##0.00`), groups bold (outermost shaded), the total on a double rule, and the groups outlined so Excel's + / - buttons fold them; sheet Choices lists the choices in words; set to print landscape on legal, one page wide. The PDF (OpenPDF, which comes with JasperReports; built in code because the columns change with the choices) is legal paper, landscape: cooperative, office, title, the choices on one line, summary boxes (outstanding, past due, at risk, interest, penalty, total owed, each bucket with its share), then the table with its header repeated on every page and page x of y at the foot; the type shrinks to fit every column (7.5 pt down to 5 pt), and the account days and dates are left out (the PDF says so) when keeping them would take it under 6.5 pt. On the whole book with every column on: 10,635 accounts, 191 pages at about 5.9 pt, both files in about 6 s after the run. Tested by `ReceivablesAgingDocumentsTest` and `ReceivablesAgingFlowTest`; not yet looked at on paper.
