# Cheque receipts (built, grant pending)

Moved from CLAUDE.md unchanged (one sentence per line). Keep this note current when the feature changes.

**Cheque receipts (built, grant pending)**: share payments, savings deposits and loan payments can be paid partly or wholly by cheque (`cheques` list on the three requests; `ChequeFields` on their dialogs; lists from `GET /api/front-office/cheque-setup`). `MemberLedgerPoster.postVoucher` writes what the old Cash Receipt wrote, mapped in `documentation/cheque-receipt-posting-map.md`: a debit per cheque on 100112/100113 (sub-ledger type 3), cash line, member credits marked `DTCOCI_TYPE` 2, cash paying the member lines first and cheques the rest as separate `sldtl` rows (`CLRG_DAYS` = kind days + 2), one `gltrans` credit per row, and `transck` rows (cash row first). **Every cash receipt of the old system has a `transck` cash row; this app's receipts now write it too.** Needs `V024_cheque_receipts.sql` (INSERT on `transck`, run by an administrator; until then cheques are refused and cash receipts skip the `transck` row); `ChequeReceiptPostingTest` skips until then.
Cheque numbers are 15 digits, dates 180 days back to 30 ahead, the same bank and number twice needs a confirmation. **Clearing is built** (`ChequeClearingController`, head cashier, accountant or administrator marks received cheques cleared in `transck.CKISCLEARED`, a reason is needed when early, audited; needs `V026_cheque_clearing.sql`; the old rule for when cheques clear is not known, see the map). **Money paid in by a cheque that is not cleared is held back from a savings account's available balance** (`ChequeRules.unclearedSql`, counted with the holds in the account list and in the withdrawal check; the old rule is unknown, so this is the app's own choice and clearing the cheque releases it).
Not built: the `sldtl.CLRG_DAYS` countdown, **Cheques can be handed between drawers together with the cash** (`cheques` on the handover request and acceptance, `cheque_amount` on `ecis_cash_handover`, `V025_cheque_handover.sql` run by an administrator; written as a second pair of lines on 100111/100112/100113, see `documentation/cheque-receipt-posting-map.md`; cheques never leave the vault except in a bank deposit), **Returned cheques are built for savings deposits** (`ChequeReturnController`, a General Journal debiting the member and crediting where the cheque is now; the poster plus a different approving supervisor via `ecis_fo_override` kind `CHEQUE_RETURN`; Returned cheque panel on the Handover tab; needs V026 for the cleared flag).
Not built: returning cheques that paid loans or share capital, cheques on payments out.
